This project presents LearNexus, an intelligent learning path advisor designed as a personalized degree navigation and continuous student support platform for post-A/L students in Sri Lanka. The study addresses the critical problem that students often lack centralized, reliable information to align their unique skills and interests with suitable higher education programs, leading to fragmented decision-making and career misalignment. The aim of the project is to develop a comprehensive system using semantic AI and knowledge graphs to help students discover appropriate degree pathways and maintain high engagement throughout their studies. The proposed system combines a modern Next.js frontend, a modular Flask backend, a Neo4j Knowledge Graph for structured data storage, and the Gemini 1.5 Flash LLM for semantic career advisory. For the machine learning component, baseline models like SVM, Random Forest, and KNN were evaluated alongside Sentence Transformers for the semantic matching of user queries to course modules. Initial implementation included web scraping university courses, extracting module content, developing interactive chat-based degree recommendations, and creating domain-restricted chatbots for specific modules. Furthermore, a WhatsApp notification engine was integrated to track user activity metrics such as login frequency and module progress, enabling the system to send proactive retention alerts after three days of inactivity. A professional development feature was also incorporated to suggest relevant free or paid LinkedIn Learning certificates tailored to the student’s specific learning path. 

The interim results show that the graph-based data pipeline, the recommendation engine, and the core frontend-backend integrations were successfully implemented, confirming the feasibility of the proposed personalized advisory workflow. The project demonstrates that an AI-driven, graph-based educational platform can be developed as a practical prototype to enhance higher education accessibility while proactively supporting student retention and professional readiness in the Sri Lankan academic landscape.
